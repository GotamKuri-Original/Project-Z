IDEA TITLE: CrimeShield AI

Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution:
  • Auto-Trace Ingestion: Connects 1930 digital complaints to bank APIs to automatically discover mule account hops.
  • 2-Stage ML Pipeline: Predicts target city (Stage 1) and exact Top 3 ATMs (Stage 2) during the 20-minute Golden Hour.
2. How it addresses the problem:
  • Eliminates the 24-hour bank-freeze latency window.
  • Replaces un-actionable city-wide heatmaps with exact, tactical street coordinates for immediate patrol dispatch.
3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution:
  • Micro-Targeting: Exact GPS coordinates rather than broad zones.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Built-in metrics explaining why an ATM was flagged (e.g., "500m from NH-19").

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
  • Plug-and-Play MHA Integration: Designed to ingest from NCRP/1930 and output to CCTNS without friction.
  • Cost-Effective: Open-source framework stack removes proprietary software licensing fees.
  • Low Hardware Overhead: XGBoost inference runs on standard police data center CPUs.
Potential challenges and risks

1. Reporting Delay: Victims reporting crime 24 hours late.

2. Data Privacy: Handling sensitive banking PII.

3. Evolving Modus Operandi: Gangs shifting territories.

Strategies for overcoming challenges

1. Adaptive Latency: Model shifts target from local ATMs to highway/border ATMs if report is delayed.

2. Strict Privacy (SHA-256): Converts accounts to cryptographic hashes. No citizen PII is ever transmitted.

3. Retraining Loop: Auto-updates risk weights based on verified CCTNS arrest data.
