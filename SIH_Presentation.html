IDEA TITLE — CrimeShield AI

❖ Proposed Solution (Official Pointers)

  • Detailed explanation of the proposed solution: An automated, API-driven predictive policing system connecting 1930/CFCFRMS complaint data to tactical physical patrol deployment. Incorporates an Automated Digital Footprint Trace that queries banking ledgers to uncover mule chain hops and destination nodes.
  • How it addresses the problem: Closes the fatal 2-to-24-hour latency window of manual bank freezing. Instead of producing vague city heatmaps (a metro has 9,000+ ATMs), our Two-Stage Pipeline isolates the Exact Top 3 High-Risk ATMs near highway escape routes.
  • Innovation and uniqueness of the solution: Micro-level physical kiosk targeting, automated chain-length discovery via banking APIs, built-in Explainable AI (XAI) feature importance, and tactical command controls (CCTV access & Geofence dispenser lock mockups).
  • Universal Cybercrime Scope: While scam methods differ (UPI fraud, fake courier scams, sextortion, investment apps), all financial syndicates use the identical mule-to-ATM cash-out funnel. CrimeShield AI protects against all of them.
🎙️ What to Speak to the Judges

"Judges, current systems rely on freezing bank accounts. But syndicates bounce money through 4 mule accounts in 10 minutes and sprint to an ATM. By the time an account is frozen, the cash is gone. Furthermore, current academic tools produce heatmaps showing that an entire city like Delhi or Mumbai is high risk. You cannot guard 9,000 ATMs! CrimeShield AI solves this. First, our Auto-Trace handshake detects the mule hops and destination bank node. Then, our Two-Stage ML engine predicts the city and isolates the exact Top 3 ATMs near highway getaways. We give police pinpoint street targets within minutes."

TECHNICAL APPROACH

1. Technologies to be used

  • Machine Learning: Python, XGBoost Classifier, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy.

2. Methodology & Implementation Flowchart

[Victim Reports 1930 / CFCFRMS] │ ▼ [Auto-Trace API: Discovers Mule Hops & Last Destination Node] │ ▼ [Stage 1: XGBoost Classifier (Predicts Target City Corridor)] │ ▼ [Stage 2: Spatial Risk Engine (Scores Highway Proximity & Blindspots)] │ ▼ [Output: Top 3 Exact High-Risk ATMs + CCTNS Patrol Dispatch Alert]
🎙️ What to Speak to the Judges

"On the technical side, we chose XGBoost because crime metadata is structured and tabular. XGBoost runs inference in under 50 milliseconds on standard CPU hardware and provides mathematically sound SHAP explainability. Our two-stage approach is key: Stage 1 predicts the general city corridor; Stage 2 scores physical factors like highway access—because runners avoid crowded market ATMs where they can get trapped; they choose ATMs within 500 meters of national expressways for immediate interstate escape."

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Analysis & Strategies (Official Pointers)

  • Analysis of Feasibility: Seamless plug-and-play integration into the Ministry of Home Affairs ecosystem. Ingests data from NCRP/1930 and outputs dispatch alerts to CCTNS. Zero proprietary software licensing fees.
  • Challenge 1 (Reporting Latency): Addressed by making reporting_delay_mins a primary model feature. Fast reports prioritize local cash-out ATMs; delayed reports shift focus to transit corridors and border ATMs.
  • Challenge 2 (Data Privacy & PII): Full compliance through SHA-256 cryptographic hashing. Citizen names and full account numbers are never exposed; only coordinates and risk scores are transmitted.
  • Challenge 3 (Evolving Gang Modus Operandi): Adaptive retraining loop ingests verified CCTNS arrest data to update spatial risk weights as syndicates shift territories.
🎙️ What to Speak to the Judges

"An AI is useless if it cannot survive in real police operations. We designed CrimeShield AI to fit between CFCFRMS (which sees the digital crime) and CCTNS (which dispatches the police). Regarding challenges: If a victim reports late, our model mathematically adapts—shifting its prediction from local city ATMs to interstate highway corridors. And we ensure complete privacy by operating on anonymized cryptographic hashes."

Examiner Defense

4 Must-Know Judge Questions

Q1: "Why predict Top 3 ATMs instead of showing a heatmap?"
Answer: "Sir, a heatmap is useful for annual reports, but useless for tactical dispatch. If we tell a police officer that South Delhi is high risk, they cannot patrol 500 ATMs. Our spatial engine isolates the exact Top 3 ATMs based on highway proximity and blindspots so a PCR van can stake out three specific spots immediately."
Q2: "How can police know the chain length when an FIR is first lodged?"
Answer: "Officers don't know it manually. That is why our platform includes an automated digital trace handshake with banking APIs. When the officer enters the complaint and clicks 'AUTO-TRACE', our system queries the inter-bank ledger in real time to extract the hop count and destination bank node automatically."
Q3: "Why include Courier Scams and Sextortion when the problem mentions UPI?"
Answer: "Because the cash-out mechanism is identical. Whether a victim is tricked via a fake courier or a UPI link, the stolen money always flows through mule accounts and is withdrawn as cash from an ATM. CrimeShield AI is a universal tool for all cyber financial fraud."
Q4: "Why XGBoost instead of Deep Learning or Neural Networks?"
Answer: "Inference latency and legal explainability. Fraud metadata is structured and tabular. Deep learning networks are slow black boxes. XGBoost executes in under 50 milliseconds on standard CPU hardware and provides exact feature importance metrics required for court evidence."
